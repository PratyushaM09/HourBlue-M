HourBlue

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Information Collected

HourBlue does not currently require visitors to create public accounts.

When a visitor voluntarily submits the subscription form, HourBlue stores the email address and the subscription creation timestamp.

Administrative access uses authentication and session cookies required for protected administration.

Purpose

Subscriber email is stored for HourBlue updates and communications related to HourBlue. HourBlue does not currently integrate with an external email-delivery provider.

Unsubscribe And Deletion

Visitors can request removal of a stored subscription email at /unsubscribe. When the submitted email matches a subscription entry, that entry is removed.

Third Parties

HourBlue uses Cloudinary to store and deliver website images. Subscriber email is not sent to Cloudinary.

The current application does not include analytics, advertising, payment, or external email-delivery integrations.

Cookies And Sessions

HourBlue uses cookies needed for security and administrative session functionality. These technical cookies are not advertising or behavioral tracking cookies.

Server And Hosting Data

Hosting or server infrastructure may process technical request information needed to operate and secure the site. HourBlue application code does not currently store public browsing profiles or browsing histories.

Data Retention

Subscriber email remains stored until removed through the unsubscribe mechanism or otherwise removed as part of site administration.

Security

HourBlue uses reasonable technical measures to protect stored information. No website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

HourBlue does not offer public accounts and does not intentionally seek personal information from children through the site.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as HourBlue changes.
